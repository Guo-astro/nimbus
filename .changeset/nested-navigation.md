- A collection served inside an autogenerated sidebar folder appears in that folder, as a group linking its landing page, so breadcrumbs and previous and next links cross between them. Its `index` page sets the group's label and order, shows only its landing (`hideChildren`) or leaves it out (`sidebar.hidden`); naming it in `sidebar.items`, or linking to its URL there, places it by hand. A docs folder whose only page is its `index` becomes a group to hold such a collection, labeled by the page's `title` rather than its `sidebar.label`. Sites without such a collection build the same sidebar.
