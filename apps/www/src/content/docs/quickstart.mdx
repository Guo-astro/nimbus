Nimbus is a documentation framework built on Astro. This page takes you from an empty folder to a published site. You need a terminal; no Nimbus or Astro knowledge is assumed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Node">
    Nimbus needs Node 22.12 or later. Check yours with `node --version`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the site">
    <PackageManagers type="create" pkg="@cloudflare/nimbus-docs@latest" />

    Answer the prompts, or press Enter for each default. The scaffolder creates `my-docs/` and installs its dependencies. Not hosting on Cloudflare? Add `--deploy other`.

    [Scaffolder options](/reference/scaffolder-options) lists every prompt and flag, including `--yes` to take every default and the empty variant with no example pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at what you got">
    <Render file="project-tree" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your site">
    Open `astro.config.ts` and set three values in the Nimbus config:

    ```ts title="astro.config.ts"
    const nimbusConfig = defineNimbusConfig({
      site: "https://docs.example.com",
      title: "Acme Docs",
      description: "Guides and reference for Acme.",
    });
    ```

    `site` is your site's address, used for canonical URLs, share cards, the sitemap and `llms.txt`. If you don't know it yet, leave the placeholder for now and set it before you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the dev server">
    ```sh
    cd my-docs
    ```

    <PackageManagers pkg="dev" type="run" />

    Open the address it prints, usually `http://localhost:4321`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a page">
    Open `src/content/docs/index.mdx`, change the title or a sentence, and save. The page in your browser updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Milestone 1: your edited page is running locally">
    You should see your site with your change, a sidebar, search in the header, and **Copy page** and **View as Markdown** above the title. To add more pages, see [Pages and routing](/writing/pages-and-routing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the site">
    <PackageManagers pkg="@cloudflare/nimbus-docs" type="exec" args="check" />

    `check` finds configuration, structure and content problems without a full build. While `site` is still the placeholder it shows a warning, not an error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Milestone 2: publish">
    **Hosting elsewhere?** Your site is already a static build. Run the build and upload `dist/` to any static host; `wrangler.jsonc` and the `deploy` script are only for Cloudflare, and you can delete them. [Deploy](/publish/deploy) covers each host, and Astro's [deploy guides](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/deploy/) cover many more.

    <PackageManagers pkg="build" type="run" />

    **On Cloudflare**, deploy:

    <PackageManagers pkg="deploy" type="run" />

    The first deploy opens a browser to sign in to Cloudflare. When it finishes, it prints your site's `*.workers.dev` URL. If you don't have your own domain yet, set `site` to that URL and deploy again, so canonical links and share cards point at the live site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next">
    Write your first real page: [Pages and routing](/writing/pages-and-routing).
  </Step>
</Steps>
