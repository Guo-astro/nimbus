By the end you'll have a docs site running locally, with a page you've edited.

## Prerequisites

- Node 22.12 or later (`node --version`)

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the site">
    <PackageManagers type="create" pkg="@cloudflare/nimbus-docs@latest" args="my-docs --yes" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the dev server">
    ```sh
    cd my-docs
    ```

    <PackageManagers pkg="dev" type="run" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the home page">
    Open `src/content/docs/index.mdx`, change the title, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You should see

```text
 astro  v<version> ready in <n> ms
┃ Local    http://localhost:4321/
┃ Network  use --host to expose
```

Open `http://localhost:4321/`: your site, with your new title, a sidebar and search. The scaffolder wrote the layouts, components and styles into `my-docs/`, and they're yours to change.
