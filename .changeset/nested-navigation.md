- A collection served inside an autogenerated sidebar folder appears in that folder, as a group linking its landing page, so breadcrumbs and previous and next links cross between them. Its `index` page sets the group's label and order, shows only its landing (`hideChildren`) or leaves it out (`sidebar.hidden`); naming it in `sidebar.items`, or linking to its URL there, places it by hand; a labeled `{ autogenerate: { collection } }` group for one links its landing page and holds the collections inside it. A page the sidebar leaves out keeps the breadcrumbs of the folders above it, and a crumb, titled like the page, for each page between there and itself. A docs folder whose only page is its `index` becomes a group to hold such a collection, keeping the page's label. Sites without such a collection build the same sidebar.
