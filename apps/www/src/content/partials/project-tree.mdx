Your pages are in `src/content/docs/`, and the components and layouts that draw them are in `src/components/` and `src/layouts/`, yours to edit. `src/styles/globals.css` holds the design tokens, and `src/components.ts` lists the components every page can use without an import. `nimbus.json` records the Nimbus version your site has caught up with; keep it committed. `wrangler.jsonc` is there only when you deploy to Cloudflare.
